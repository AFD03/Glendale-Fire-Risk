Why Glendale? — Regional Context

Glendale is located at the intersection of several high-risk wildfire environments: the Verdugo Mountains, Angeles National Forest, and the northern Los Angeles foothills. This region experiences prolonged drought cycles, Santa Ana wind events, and some of the highest wildfire activity in Southern California.

The graphs below summarize key regional factors that contribute to Glendale’s structural wildfire risk. These provide context for the building-level 2D and 3D tools available on the other pages.

Wildfire Frequency in LA county

The Glendale area is surrounded by three of California’s most fire-prone landscapes: Los Angeles County wildlands, Ventura County fire corridors, and Angeles National Forest. These regions regularly experience large fires that impact Glendale's air quality, evacuation planning, and building resilience needs.
